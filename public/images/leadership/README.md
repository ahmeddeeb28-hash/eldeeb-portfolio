# Leadership & Strategy — card images

**Component:** `components/LeadershipStrategy.tsx`

Each of the six cards shows one image as a hint of what is inside. Until an
image is set, the card shows a built-in placeholder.

| Card | Suggested filename |
|------|--------------------|
| 01 Product & Design Strategy           | `01-strategy.jpg`   |
| 02 Design Operations & Leadership      | `02-operations.jpg` |
| 03 Design Systems & Governance         | `03-systems.jpg`    |
| 04 Product Analytics & Business Impact | `04-analytics.jpg`  |
| 05 Research & Decision Frameworks      | `05-research.jpg`   |
| 06 Leadership Playbooks & Case Studies | `06-playbooks.jpg`  |

- Landscape, 16:10 (e.g. 1600 × 1000). JPG or WebP, under ~250 KB.
- This folder is public. Use images that are safe to show anyone, such as a
  cropped or blurred artefact, never the confidential document itself.

After adding a file, set the card's `image` in `AREAS`, for example
`image: '/images/leadership/01-strategy.jpg'`.
